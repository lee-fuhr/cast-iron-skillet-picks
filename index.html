How we choose our picks

We don’t chase lab numbers or run timed searing tests in a studio. Instead, we dig into what actually matters after the first year: how the seasoning holds up, whether the handle stays comfortable, and how the pan handles everyday abuse. Our recommendations come from combing through decades of owner forums, long-term durability reports, and published reviews from trusted culinary sources, not from a single afternoon of frying bacon. We pay close attention to failure points: warping, rust spots that won’t go away, handles that get dangerously hot, and finishes that flake. The skillets listed here have a proven track record of avoiding those issues, with the vast majority of owners reporting satisfaction over years of heavy use. If a product has a pattern of complaints about seasoning failure or cracking, it doesn’t make the cut, no matter how cheap or popular it is.
