Cast iron skillets are the workhorses of the kitchen, they sear, fry, bake, and braise with unmatched heat retention, and they only get better with age. But not every pan is built the same. The best cast iron skillet for you depends on how you cook, your budget, and how much weight you’re willing to heft. When choosing a skillet, look at size, handle design, surface smoothness, and overall weight. A 10- or 12-inch pan handles most meals, while a rougher cooking surface (common on budget models) requires a longer seasoning break-in. Pros

  • Outstanding heat retention and even heating across the pan

Our verdict

Cast iron skillets come with real trade-offs in weight, surface finish, and price. For the vast majority of home cooks, the Lodge 12-inch hits the perfect sweet spot of performance, durability, and value, it‘s the pan you’ll reach for again and again. If you want something lighter, smoother, or more decorative, the runner-up picks above each serve a specific purpose without sacrificing quality. No matter which you choose, a well-cared-for cast iron skillet will reward you with decades of great meals.
